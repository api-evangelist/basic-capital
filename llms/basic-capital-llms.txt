# Basic Capital

> Basic Capital is a modern 401(k) platform that lets participants finance assets inside their retirement plan — offering $4 of financing for every $1 contributed for up to 5x investing power — plus access to alternative and private-market investments, 6,000+ investment options, a built-in brokerage window, mega backdoor Roth support, and its "Retirement Mortgage" product. It serves employers, employees, and advisors, with an IRA offering for individuals. HQ New York City; $25M Series A (2025) led by Forerunner and Lux Capital. Basic Capital does not publish a public developer API; this profile tracks the company for the API Evangelist network.
